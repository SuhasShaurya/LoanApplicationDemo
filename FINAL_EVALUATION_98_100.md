## Lessons Learned

### What Worked Well
1. **Phase-based approach** - Clear milestones and deliverables
2. **Comprehensive testing** - Caught issues early
3. **Documentation as you go** - Made final deployment smoother
4. **LLM integration thoughtfully** - Extended thinking and refinement add real value
5. **Observability from start** - Operational visibility is critical

### Recommendations for Future
1. **Start monitoring earlier** - Phase 6 insights would help earlier phases
2. **Load test continuously** - Not just at the end
3. **Security audit earlier** - Good to validate design assumptions
4. **User feedback loops** - Validate decision quality with stakeholders
5. **A/B testing framework** - Compare LLM vs rule-based decisions
