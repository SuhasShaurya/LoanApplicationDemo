KEY FEATURES IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================

SCORING ALGORITHM:
  • Weight 35%: Credit Risk Score (most important)
  • Weight 25%: Income Stability Score
  • Weight 25%: Debt-to-Income Ratio
  • Weight 10%: Employment Risk Score
  • Weight 5%:  Loan Amount Risk Score
  
  Formula: Overall Score = Σ(factor_value × weight)
  
  Decision Thresholds:
    - Score < 0.35  → APPROVE (Confidence: 0.85-0.95, Risk: 0.10-0.20)
    - Score 0.35-0.55 → REVIEW (Confidence: 0.60-0.75, Risk: 0.40-0.60)
    - Score > 0.55  → REJECT (Confidence: 0.80-0.95, Risk: 0.70-0.95)
