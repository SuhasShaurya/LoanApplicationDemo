================================================================================
QUICK REFERENCE - WHAT EACH STATUS MEANS
================================================================================

✅ APPROVED
   • All criteria met
   • Strong financial profile
   • Low default risk
   • Proceed to fund disbursement
   • Applicant gets loan

❌ REJECTED
   • Does not meet criteria
   • Multiple risk factors
   • High default risk
   • Cannot be approved
   • Applicant must reapply after improvement

⚠️ UNDER REVIEW
   • Mixed signals
   • Needs human review
   • Could go either way
   • Underwriter will decide
   • Takes 2-3 business days

METRICS EXPLAINED
================================================================================

RISK SCORE (0.0 - 1.0)
├─ 0.00-0.35  →  ✅ Low Risk      →  APPROVE
├─ 0.35-0.55  →  ⚠️ Moderate     →  REVIEW
└─ 0.55-1.00  →  ❌ High Risk     →  REJECT

CONFIDENCE (0% - 100%)
├─ 0-60%      →  Low (uncertain)
├─ 60-80%     →  Medium (sure)
├─ 80-95%     →  High (very sure)
└─ 95-100%    →  Very High (certain)

DTI RATIO (Debt-to-Income)
├─ < 0.30     →  ✅ Healthy (can afford)
├─ 0.30-0.45  →  ✅ Acceptable (good)
├─ 0.45-0.60  →  ⚠️ High (tight)
└─ > 0.60     →  ❌ Unaffordable (cannot pay)

CREDIT SCORE
├─ 750+       →  ✅ Excellent (very low risk)
├─ 700-749    →  ✅ Good (low risk)
├─ 650-699    →  ⚠️ Fair (medium risk)
├─ 600-649    →  ⚠️ Poor (high risk)
└─ < 600      →  ❌ Very Poor (very high risk)
