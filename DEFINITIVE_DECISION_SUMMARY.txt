================================================================================
DECISION THRESHOLD
================================================================================

Single Threshold: 0.45

Composite Risk Score Calculation:
  Score = (Credit 0.35) + (Income 0.25) + (DTI 0.25) 
         + (Employment 0.10) + (Loan 0.05)

Decision Logic:
  if Score < 0.45  →  ✅ APPROVE
  if Score ≥ 0.45  →  ❌ REJECT

No Gray Area:
  • No values fall into a "Review" zone
  • Every score maps to Approve or Reject
  • Clear, definitive decisions

================================================================================
APPROVAL CRITERIA (Score < 0.45)
================================================================================

Application is APPROVED when:

✅ Financial Health
  • DTI Ratio: < 0.30-0.45 (can afford)
  • Credit Score: 700+ (good history)
  • Income Stability: 0.75+ (strong)

✅ Employment & Loan
  • Employment Risk: Low (stable)
  • Loan Risk: Low to Medium
  • Loan-to-Income: ≤ 3.0x

✅ Overall Score
  • Combined risk score < 0.45
  • Low probability of default

Example: Approved Applicant
  Age 35, Income $85,000, Full-time, Credit 760
  DTI: 0.11, Loan: $200,000
  Score: 0.15 (< 0.45)  →  ✅ APPROVED

================================================================================
REJECTION CRITERIA (Score ≥ 0.45)
================================================================================

Application is REJECTED when:

❌ Financial Issues
  • DTI Ratio: > 0.45 (difficult to afford)
  • Credit Score: < 650 (poor history)
  • Income Instability: < 0.5 (unreliable)

❌ High Risk Factors
  • Employment Risk: High (unstable)
  • Loan Risk: High or Very High
  • Loan-to-Income: > 3.0x

❌ Overall Score
  • Combined risk score ≥ 0.45
  • High probability of default

Examples:

1. Rejected - Multiple Issues
   Age 28, Income $35,000, Self-employed, Credit 550
   DTI: 0.77, Loan: $280,000
   Score: 0.91 (> 0.45)  →  ❌ REJECTED

2. Rejected - Mixed Signals
   Age 32, Income $55,000, Part-time, Credit 680
   DTI: 0.42, Loan: $150,000
   Score: 0.47 (> 0.45)  →  ❌ REJECTED
   (Mixed signals now reject, not review)

3. Rejected - High DTI
   Age 45, Income $60,000, Full-time, Credit 700
   DTI: 0.65, Loan: $250,000
   Score: 0.62 (> 0.45)  →  ❌ REJECTED
